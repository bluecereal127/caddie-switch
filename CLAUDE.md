# Caddie·Switch — project context for Claude Code

Personal solver/tracker for Nintendo Switch Sports golf (21 holes). Single-page
React app (Vite). No backend; all state in localStorage via `src/storage.js`
(shim over the claude.ai artifact storage API). App.jsx forked from artifact
v13 and now diverges (v14: capture catalog; 2026-10-24/25: the NSS changeover);
the web repo is the source of truth.

**The NSS changeover** (the user, 2026-10-24: "we are replacing the WSR data
with our new NSS data from this point forward"). Everything the app ships about
the course and the ball is Nintendo Switch Sports' own data (NSS 1.5.0, the
user's dump): the six cups per hole (16 marked difficult), the local tee bone,
the online platform and its seating rule, the collision meshes (terrain, live
solver), the minimap art, the trees and the ball physics (decoded from the
game's code and RomFS, confirmed against strokes recorded from Eden's RAM).
Wii Sports Resort's data (the KCL meshes, the PMP placements, `src/wsrsim.js`,
the Dolphin harness and its playbook) stays in the repo as history and for the
harness; it no longer feeds the app, except as an audit (which NSS cups
coincide with WSR spots) and through the frame NSS shares with it (NSS's world
is WSR's mesh frame / 10 with a per-hole offset). The playbook card is gone:
shots are computed from NSS's physics on NSS's meshes and cups.

**The eden dump** (git-ignored, read-only): `tools/harness/state/eden/nss-golf-x/`
holds the decodes and their evidence: `REPORT.md` (the consolidated decode and
verification), `INTEGRATION.md` (the per-file design, decisions D1–D10;
superseded where this file differs: the tee, the playbook, the maps, trees and
physics all moved to NSS since), `physics.md` / `physics.json` (the ball),
`GolfShotVelTable.json` (the launch-speed table read from Eden's RAM:
authoritative), `flights/` + `flights.md` (71 strokes recorded tick by tick),
`launch.json`, `swing-power.md` (the swing → launch power chain and its
assists), `slots.md` / `slots.json` (the platform's seating), `pins.md` /
`pins.json`, `platform.md`, `trees.md` / `trees.json`, `collision/` (the meshes),
`ball/` (the extracted GolfBall / GolfPlayer packs) and `verify/` (each
verifier's corrections, which override the decodes). The RomFS is
`tools/harness/state/eden/nss-romfs/romfs` (BYML through
`tools/harness/state/eden/tools/byml.py`, run with `python -P`), the decoders
`tools/harness/state/eden/tools/*.py`. Generators that read the dump run on
this machine and commit their outputs (as the WSR generators did with the KCL
rasters); build-derived and every test run from committed files (a test's dump steps skip without the dump).

## Files
- `src/App.jsx` — the app (~2630 lines): solver tab (under the distance, the ball's height against the tee and the target's against the ball, from the terrain; a tapped rec card marks its landing ◆ beside its stop ● on the map, and a rec whose flight goes into a trunk or loses a shot's worth through a canopy, badged 🌲 TREE, rings its trunk; on the green lie the putting panel: the putt read with its gauge bars, the slope source with "use mesh heights" after an edit, and "Log a flat putt", which fits greenSpeed), hole maps, greens, rounds, log, clubs, guide (per-hole setup steps with an auto-tracked progress table, and shot-data coverage); the Holes tab's ⚙ has a "Contours (debug)" chip that lays src/relief.js's contour lines over the hole map (component state, not stored). Pins are NSS's six cups (`pinTitle`: "NSS cup N", "a difficult cup (red)" on the 16, the yards from the tee, the rounds seen): the zoomed green (GreenView) numbers all six, a difficult cup with a red number and ring, the pin in use filled red; on the hole map during play only the pin in use is drawn (a difficult cup's flag red, ringed at its foot), all six in the Holes tab's pin edit. Under the map the "Online platform" card (src/platform.js): an Online toggle, My tier High / Low, Players high 1–4, Players low 0 / 2 / 3 / 4, My place from the left, I swing Right- / Left-handed; the ball's place depends only on the own tier, its count, the place and the hand (slots.md section 1), so a count chip keeps the tapped tier's count and moves the other tier's to the nearest count NSS seats beside it (`pairWith`: 2 high → 0 low, 2 low → 3 high); a stored pair NSS never seats (2 high + 4 low) is kept, flagged with the counts NSS seats beside the player's own and a one-tap fix that keeps the own tier and count ("Use 2 high + 0 low"), while the ball stands on the own tier's place all the same; a low tier with nobody on it keeps the ball on the tee and offers "I'm on the high tier"; a hole without platform data says so. "⛳ From tee" reads "⛳ From platform" while Online is on; a new hole, and a change of the start itself (Online toggled, derived.json arriving late) while the ball is unplaced or on a start, puts the ball on `startOf` (the platform ball, else derived.json's `tee`); the map rings the ball whenever it sits on a platform spot; `ballLift` = the tier's mat less the terrain under the ball, from where the ball is, not from the toggle (0.109 yd low, 1.824 yd high on a flat pad). The playbook card (`PlaybookCard`) is gone (2026-10-24; src/playbook.js stays, unused). Under the zoomed green the live solve card (`LiveSolveCard`, through src/live3d.js; titled "🎯 Live solve · " + `COURSE_LABEL` + ", " + `PHYSICS_LABEL`): props hole, line, `cupKey` (c0..c5 of the cup in use), pin, the dial's wind, `start` ({ kind: "local" } on the tee, { kind: "platform", tier, hand, ballLocalX } on the platform, else null) and `lift`; its result goes stale when any of them changes; a picked shot draws on the map and the green (`liveRoute`). The solver's map header has "Map fit / 3x / 4x" chips, the map's magnification: the setting `mapScale` (2, 3 or 4, default 2) is stored at the root of the saved state. 2 = fit, the whole hole in the panel from src/maps.js's 2x image. 3 and 4 draw the map 1.5× and 2× the panel's width (the names follow the render scales; the help line under the chips says so) in a viewport of the fitted map's size (HoleMap's `zoom` = mapScale / 2): a touch pans it natively (`touch-action: pan-x pan-y`, `overscroll-behavior: contain`), a mouse drags it (captured past 4 CSS px, so a click stays a tap) or wheels it. It opens on the ball with the target in view (src/mapView.js `scrollFor`), or, with no target drawn, on the hole's default line to the pin in use (as `defaultBearing` has it), so a hole opened magnified shows the ball at the bottom margin and the fairway running up the view; placed again on a new magnification, hole or ball, on a target set outside the map (🎯 Target = pin, the distance field) and on a change of the pin in use while no target is drawn, never on the map's own target tap or drag, so the user's panning stays; a ball the map set itself glides there. A resize (the phone turned) keeps the view's centre where it was on the map (a ResizeObserver scales the scroll offsets), since the map and the view scale with the panel. Taps are measured on the image's own rect, so they need no scroll offset; the marks keep their fitted size on screen (drawn at 1 / zoom about their spots), the tap rules' reaches (the target grab, 0.07 of the map; own-marker removal, 0.06) shrink with zoom to stay the same on screen and the pin hit test follows the drawn pin, and a grab handle with `touch-action: none` over the target keeps the target drag on a touch. The image is the public/maps/ file at that scale (`useHoleMapSrc`, through src/mapScale.js), shown once a preload of it has loaded, so the 2x image shows (magnified) first and stays when a file is missing or fails (the header then says so). The Holes tab's ⚙ map stays fitted (whole hole, for editing) but takes the same image, as do both green canvases; the hole thumbnails stay 2x. At fit the map's DOM is what it was before the magnified view. The shot model itself is `src/model.js`.

## Model
**NSS's ball** (src/nsssim.js, constants tools/nss-physics.json), decoded from
NSS 1.5.0's code and RomFS and confirmed tick by tick on 71 strokes recorded
from Eden's RAM (dump `physics.md`, `flights.md`; the verifiers' corrections in
`verify/flights/verdicts.json` override the decode). Units m, s; the frame is
the NSS hole frame (+x east, +y up, −z toward the minimap's north).
- Tick 1/240 s (4 per 60 Hz frame, dt = the game's 0.016666668 / 4); the ball
  state is float32.
- Launch: speed = `GolfShotVelTable[club][pct]`, pct = (int)(100·clamp(power,
  0.01, 1.5)) in float32 (System/CombinationDataTableData/GolfShotVelTable, read
  from Eden's RAM: 8 clubs WD1 WD3 IR3 IR5 IR7 IR9 SW PT × Power1..150, m/s; at
  100 % 57.6 / 54.4 / 54.2 / 48.3 / 42.5 / 36.1 / 25.6 / 8.52; every recorded
  launch equals it within 1.4e-4, every repeat identical, no interpolation),
  pitched up `LoftDeg` 10.5 / 12 / 25 / 28 / 29 / 34 / 40 / 1.14° along the aim;
  hop = `HopSpinRatio` 3.0 for the woods, 1.0 for the rest, not scaled by power;
  side 0 on a straight swing. Lies only cap the power (`RoughRatio` 0.75;
  `BunkerRatio` 0.25 woods / 0.5 irons / 0.75 wedge): the speed is the fairway
  table's at the capped power. The table was built so a calm flat carry is
  pct / 100 × `CarryMaxYard` (240 / 220 / 190 / 160 / 130 / 100 / 55, WSR's
  nominal carries) within 0.99–1.035, the putter's roll pct / 100 × 20 yd.
- Air (mode 1), after the move: gravity 10 m/s² (+ `AddGravityHole` 8 ·
  Hermite((0.5, 1), (1, 0))(d / 0.3) within 0.3 m of the cup, in flight too);
  drag v −= 0.0025 / 4 · v a tick; lift 0.0012 · hop · |u| / 4 along
  normalize(−ux uy, ux² + uz², −uy uz) on the post-move velocity u; hop ×=
  0.999632597 a tick (the game's approximate pow, read in RAM; not 0.9985^¼);
  wind |W| = s (linear; s a whole 0..15 m/s), × WindEffectCurve 0.2 up to 3 m
  above the launch rising to 1 at 30 m, v += 0.33 (W across + 0.6 W along u's
  heading) dt, shots only; side spin `SideSpinK` 0.0015 (the law is in, untested:
  every recorded swing was straight); under the water line v −= 0.1 v / 4 a
  tick; inside a Leaf canopy (pass-through) each tick keeps 1 − 0.03 / 4 of the
  horizontal and rising vertical speed (a counter with a 0.5 s timer); a
  PalmLeaf contact keeps 1 − 0.08 a tick; trunks (GolfTree) are solid.
- Bounce: v' = ReflectionH · vt − ReflectionV · k · vn per surface (Fairway
  0.6 / 0.37, Green 0.5 / 0.37, Rough 0.53 / 0.32, Bunker 0.27 / 0.10, OB 0.40 /
  0.24, fringe 0.6 / 0.37, Rock 0.5 / 0.5, SandOB 0.4 / 0.1, Tree 0.4 / 0.1;
  Cupside caps v.y at 2 m/s), backspin k = 1 − 0.4 b^1.737; the contact point is
  the centre less 0.05 m along the normal (the Phive shape says 0.056).
- Roll switch: a shot touching ground rolls (mode 2) when the incoming normal
  speed, the pass-through contact's speed and |vin.y| are all under
  `FieldResistanceEnableSpeed` 4 m/s (the third condition is the verifiers'
  correction); a roll airborne for 0.2 s is a shot again; at the switch the
  backspin pull-back P = 28 · b^0.737 · max(d·a, 0) is stored.
- Roll tick: slide (the into-plane velocity removed), drag, friction |v| −=
  Friction[attr] · FrictionRatioCurve(|v|) · dt (Fairway 3.5, Green 1.3, Rough
  7, fringe 2.6, Bunker 10, OB 8; the ratio 1 to 5 m/s, 0.75 at 7.5, 0.5 from
  10), the pull-back v += r · P · dt (r = BackSpinAngleRatioCurve of the angle
  between P and the ball-to-cup direction, 1.3 → 1.0: pin homing; P decays
  0.98745 a tick on the green), the stop count (horizontal |v| ≤ 0.005 m/s for
  10 frames is a rest), then gravity: 6 on the green (`GravityPutter`), 10
  elsewhere; a slow ball (|v| ≤ 0.5 m/s, n.y ≥ 0.5) more than 1 m from the cup
  takes it along −n, so it feels no slope. Force stops at 1300 frames, below y
  −70, or within 0.2 m of one spot for 180 frames. The putter launches in mode
  2 (its 1.14° loft hops it a few ticks on a flat green).
- The cup is NSS's collision: a 20-sided hole, its rim 0.193 m round on the
  green (following its slope), a 0.043 m bevel to the 0.15 m wall, 0.31 m deep
  (the medians of the 126). Recorded holes entered at 1.08–2.47 m/s; h08s6's
  lip-out crossed at 2.86 m/s, 0.125 m off centre.
- Replays (tools/test-nsssim.mjs): flights within 0.1 mm, rests off the cup
  within 0.04 m, every holed putt holed. Not modelled: the flagstick and pin (a
  flag hit zeroes v, then deflects randomly), side spin from a curve,
  over-swing randomness, the swing's backspin source (b is an input), the
  green-substitution flags; the HUD's mph is approximate (below). On about 1 %
  of rolling ticks (a roll-off over a convex edge) the game's contact differs.
- Level ground, calm (src/nssShape.js): full-power carry / stop on fairway
  240.3 / 265.6 (driver), 220.4 / 244.7, 190.5 / 218.3, 160.5 / 184.7, 130.2 /
  151.6, 100.4 / 117.5, 55.3 / 66.1 yd (wedge); apexes 25.5 / 24.8 / 30.7 / 29.0
  / 23.8 / 22.0 / 14.3 yd. The stop is not monotone in power: where a landing's
  normal speed crosses the roll switch the ball takes one bounce more and the
  stop steps DOWN (the driver on fairway 196.8 yd at 67 %, 186.7 at 68 %; 14
  such steps on fairway, each one bounce more; the driver's largest over the
  surfaces 42.6 yd).

**The solver** (src/model.js): stop = 4a · (1 + h · wAlong) · S(bars/4,
landing surface); carry = 4a · (1 + h · wAlong) · C(bars/4); S, C are NSS's
(src/nssShape.js) normalised by the full-power fairway stop, so S(1, fairway) =
1 and `a` is the 4-bar fairway stop ÷ 4. Distances are STOP distances (carry +
roll).
- The table is linear between whole percents (the game flies the lower one:
  up to 0.04 bar short, the driver's 2.3 yd at full power, the wedge's 0.7).
- Landing surface (Amendment 2, 2026-10-20): the App reads the class along the
  shot line from public/terrain (`ctx.surfaceAt`), and per club the solver scans
  f(p) = the stop of power p on the surface its own carry lands on
  (`landedStop`) from 0.4 bars to the cap every 0.02 bar (every other sample a
  table knot), each change of surface between two samples bisected; f is cut
  into its rising pieces (a fall, NSS's bounce step, is a piece of its own and
  dropped). A distance has a solution on each run whose ends straddle it (the
  best score wins, ties to the lower power) and none inside a jump (up onto a
  green, down into a bunker, a bounce more: "No club stops there"). `maxReach`
  is the top of f. Water, OB, walls and a missing terrain roll as fairway; the
  card names water, OB or a bank at the carry. The power bar maps through the
  same f (`stopAt`).
- Wind and elevation (since 2026-10-25 NSS's own flight): with wind on the dial
  or the terrain's heights (`ctx.heightAt`, the ground along the line relative
  to the ground under the ball) each club's table solution is refined by flying
  NSS's ball over the line's profile (`simProfile`: heightAt, attrAt in NSS
  metres, every 0.25 yd), the wind in the air by NSS's law: a Newton step on
  the table's slope, then secant steps through the last two flights inside the
  bracket they narrow, exact within 0.5 yd, else the closest flight flagged
  `approx` and ranked after the exact recs. The sim's horizontal distances
  scale by k = 4a (1 + h · wAlong) / stopYd (1 for a seed club), heights
  unscaled. The dial's mph becomes NSS's wind speed s by inverting floor(2.25 s)
  when a whole s shows that mph, else mph / 2.25 (approximate: the HUD's
  rounding is not decoded). Drift is the flight's own lateral (aim = −drift)
  plus the fitted residual c · wCross · bars. A rec adds `stop`, `dh` / `dhStop`
  (the ground at the carry / the stop less the ball's, yd, + uphill), `elev`,
  `approx`. A flight costs about 0.5 ms; budget 40 ms a 7-club solve in Node
  (14.8 ms mean calm, 18.5 in a 13 mph crosswind).
- `ctx.ballLift`: the ball's height above the ground under it, 0 on the course;
  on the platform the tier's mat less the terrain (0.109 yd low, 1.824 yd high
  on a flat pad; H3 0.219 / 1.934, its platform origin 0.1 m up); a tee shot
  launches from a tee 0.1 m up (every recorded tee shot did).
- Backspin: `rollSpin` is null, so the cards show no spin range: NSS's swing
  sets b (swing-power.md: 0 for driver, spoon and putter; otherwise it needs a
  short finish and a strong swing; decoded, not tested), which the app cannot
  know.
- Powers stay inside [0.4, cap]: caps stay hard, and `red` (power ≥ cap − 0.1)
  warns that past the cap the bar goes red and the ball curves to a random side.
- Trees (NSS's since 2026-10-25): with the terrain loaded the App hands the
  solver `ctx.trees` (src/trees.js `treesAlong`: the hole's tree markers on the
  line's frame, `off` trees included, each base's height against the ground
  under the ball; the player's own markers as a median broadleaf on the ground
  under them). Every club with a rec flies once more at its solved power,
  traced (`flightOf`: NSS's path every 1/60 s with its dt and ballZ), and
  `treeCheck` tests it against each tree's collision: a trunk or limb (tapered
  capsules, tested segment by segment) ends the shot (kind "trunk": the bounce
  keeps 0.4 along the bark, returns 0.1); a canopy only slows the ball (Leaf:
  0.9925 a tick of the horizontal and rising speed for at most 120 ticks after
  each entry, another sphere of the same crown resetting the limit; palm and
  branch sheets 0.92 a tick of contact, the cover fraction as the chance of
  touching one), reported as kind "canopy" with `keep`, `lossYd` (an estimate
  of how much shorter it finishes) and the passes when it costs at least
  `CLIP_YD` 1 yd, else a graze reported over / under with a negative margin;
  past a kept speed of 0.75 later trees are not checked. A hit rec gets `tree =
  { kind, at, id, shape, own, alt }` and the penalty (below every clean rec,
  above a manual block's +10); `alt` = the best clean rec and how it passes
  that tree. The card shows 🌲 TREE (the manual "🌲 BLOCKED" is separate and
  still wins); a tapped hit card rings the trunk; the aim preview skips hit
  recs. A canopy pass costs about 10–50 yd by these rules (H7's drive from
  GlfTeeOBJ through the off-course six-sphere crown t7-37: 10–17 yd short).
  ON_PATH, CLIP_YD and lossYd are heuristics to tune once real shots are
  logged. The merge carries each tree's `col` (TREE_FIELDS), so every NSS tree is
  checked against its own collision; a marker without it (a player's own, or
  an install whose derived.json has not loaded) against its family's template
  (TREE_SHAPES) at its size and sy. 7-club solve with trees 18.6 ms mean / 25.6
  p90 on H7, 19.0 / 28.0 on H19 (test-trees; budget 40 ms, test-solver's).
- Fitted (Switch): a, h and c per club from the logged shots, each row read
  through NSS's own level flight at its power, where it finished (`landed`,
  fairway when absent) and in its own wind, so h and c are the residuals
  over NSS's wind (0 when the logs agree); a pipeline row with `flatEnded` is
  read as that level-fairway stop. A club with no rows keeps NSS's numbers. The
  logs agree with NSS: the 29 rows of 2026-08-21 flown from their frames' spots
  over NSS's terrain in their wind stop a median 0.5 yd from what was logged
  (p90 3.1, worst 7.3); hole 10's four clean full-power driver tee shots
  274.1 / 281.0 / 284.3 / 293.1 against 274 / 281 / 284 / 292 logged. Fits
  prefer tee/fairway shots; null sideways entries are skipped.
- Not in the model yet: over-swing power, Shot Assist.

**Putts** (src/putt.js, since 2026-10-25 NSS's roll; decision D3 of 2026-10-20

## Confirmed game mechanics (do not regress these)
- Gauge = 4 bars, linear (power = bars / 4; a = the 4-bar stop / 4). The launch speed is NSS's GolfShotVelTable at the swing's whole percent, so carry is linear in power to ±3.5 % and the stop is not ("Model"). The seed clubs are NSS's own (src/model.js DEFAULT_CLUBS: 265.6 / 244.7 / 218.3 / 184.7 / 151.6 / 117.5 / 66.1 yd full-power stops on level fairway, driver to wedge), and the logs fit them (hole 10's drives within 1.1 yd, the 29 rows a median 0.5 yd).
- The swing's launch power (swing-power.md, decoded and checked in RAM): a backswing assist pulls the downswing's power to the gauge's white line (the top of the backswing: 4 bars at 150°, 0 at 15°) — exactly onto it within 0.05 of power, part way to 0.5, none beyond (a higher-handicap curve for a player well over par) — then the lie's cap and the clamp to [PowerMin 0.08, PowerMax]; the putter instead gets the putter assist: while the player addresses the ball the game runs ghost putts with its own ball on the real green along the current aim and keeps a target power (the middle of the holing range on the hundredth grid; the closest miss's when the aim never holes; none when that miss runs over 75° off the cup's direction), and the swing is pulled toward it (with Shot Assist exactly onto it within 0.15 of power, 0.25 within 7 m; without, within 0.03), within 25 m of the cup (30 with Shot Assist or a high handicap), never from the fringe or on a practice swing.
- Lies cap the usable BAR; distance per bar is unchanged below the cap (the speed is the fairway table's at the capped power). Rough = 3 bars (all clubs; RoughRatio 0.75). Sand: wedge 3, irons 2, spoon/driver 1 (player-confirmed; NSS's BunkerRatio 0.75 / 0.5 / 0.25, BUNKER_CAPS). Putter uncapped, dead roll in sand.
- Driver & spoon can NEVER backspin (player-confirmed; the swing's backspin code gives 0 for WD1, WD3 and the putter). Spin strength scales with swing power (official; the decode's backspin rises with the assisted power and needs a short finish) → capped lies also cap spin.
- Wind: exactly 8 directions (N/NE/E/SE/S/SW/W/NW: dir × 45°), NSS's speed s a whole 0..15 m/s drawn from 16 weights, linear in its effect. The HUD shows integer mph, observed 0–31 (odd values exist; WSR was even-only 0–30): about 2.24 s (floor(2.25 s) fits all six (s, mph) pairs Eden recorded, 1→2, 3→6, 4→9, 5→11, 6→13, 10→22; the captures' 17 mph fits floor(2.237 s), the code's converter, instead; the rounding is not decoded). 12 and 23 mph, never captured, are values no whole s gives. Arrow colour, measured on the 317 map captures with an arrow: blue through 11 mph, lime at 13–22, pink from 24 (s ≤ 5 / 6–10 / ≥ 11; the app's dials split at 12 and 24). At 0 mph the dial shows no arrow; direction is still readable from the pin flag.
- Pins (tools/PINS.md): NSS places exactly six cups per hole, the bones `GolfCourseNNhole0..5` of the course model (verified five independent ways: the bones, the cup mesh, the six holes cut in each green mesh, the 126 HoleCover packs, the collision cups). The game's `CupDifficulties` (GolfFieldMgrParam) marks 16 difficult: H5 5, H6 6, H7 5, H8 4, H9 3, H14 6, H16 1 and 5, H17 1, H18 4, H19 2 and 6, H20 3 and 5, H21 1 and 6 (labels = k + 1). The draw: a settings byte 0 → cup 0; otherwise the candidate cups are Fisher–Yates shuffled (xorshift128, a per-hole seed) and the last becomes the cup in play; online (inferred: mode 1) a player rated under `DifficultCupRatingThreshold` (code fallback 500) gets the non-difficult cups only, and a mode-0 score test picks only the difficult or only the others (decoded from code, not yet seen in play). Only 7 of the 126 coincide with WSR's spots (0.01–0.24 yd); the rest stand a median 2.8 yd from the nearest (max 11.5); "NSS reuses WSR's spots" was wrong, and the captures' Switch-only spots (H10 ×2, H18, H21) and H1's 384 yd candidate are NSS cups. The local "yd to go" badge is floor(flat yd from GlfTeeOBJ to the cup).
- Tees: local play tees off EXACTLY at the course bone `GlfTeeOBJ`: the game copies it into every player's start matrix on every hole (main 0xd549ac..), measured in Eden's RAM at 0.00 yd on holes 2–18, the ball teed up 0.1 m (all 17 recorded tee shots). The ground under the decks is level with the platform origin on 18 holes (dipping to −0.05 m on H12, −0.1 on H18; on H3 the origin stands 0.1 m above it), and on H1 the outer slots stand over GolfOB ground. The 13 judged online rounds' badges each fit a standing spot of their tier (tools/test-platform.mjs). The raised tier doesn't shift the minimap marker.
- Over-swinging (the red past the lie's cap) curves the ball to a RANDOM side on every swing (player-confirmed 2026-10-19; NSS sets an over-swing flag and the excess past the cap feeds a random curve, 0xe8d63c, not decoded). NSS's PowerMax: 1.0 on tee / fairway / green (no over-swing power there), cap + 0.25 in the rough and sand (rough 1.0, iron bunker 0.75, wood bunker 0.5, wedge bunker 1.0), the putter 1.5. The bar's wobble is display only. The solver treats caps as hard.
- Shot Assist (Options → User Settings → Other; local play only) locks the swing perfectly straight AND allows maxing the bars — player-confirmed; decoded: it caps the power at the lie's cap (no over-swing) and forces the curve to 0 (swing+0x110 / 0x111, verified in RAM), and puts the putter assist in its widest mode. It's the calibration tool.
- Minimap meter dots = flat-terrain carry; elevation shifts real distance (notably holes 8, 12, 17).
- Community wind-route rules encoded in WIND_RULES (H7/10/12/14/15/16/18/21).
- Restarting a hole (local play) restarts its 3-hole track with IDENTICAL wind. Outcomes are deterministic: same club/power/aim/wind → pixel-identical ball position (player-confirmed with driver, 9i, and putter across restarts).
- Every green is ringed by a FRINGE (player-confirmed; NSS's GolfGreenEdge): a band roughly the
  colour of fairway but distinct, wrapping the entire green without a break.
  Outside it is fairway or rough — never water, never OB dark rough. The
  fringe's inner edge is therefore a true polygon boundary for the green
  (the grid-squared, lighter surface inside it), and a far better mask than
  any bounding box. Terrain view recolours only the green itself, not the
  fringe, so the fringe survives in both plain and heightmap frames.
- Greens are drawn with a visible GRID of squares on the putting surface —
  a known-scale lattice (NSS's plaid: 2 m tiles on every hole, measured on
  six holes' zoomed frames), so it doubles as a registration cue when aligning
  green captures taken at different zoom levels.
- Green B-grid is a STATIC shaded overlay — lighter = higher, darker = lower (game8 guide + player confirmation; NSS's Green capture texture's height bands). No flowing dots/motion, unlike some other golf games.
- Putter bar shows no fill during the backstroke but displays the final fill once the putt is made (player-confirmed) — post-stroke capture frames work for putts too.
- All in-game distance readouts ("NNN yd to go", distance-to-pin) are FLAT level-ground distances — elevation never enters them (player-confirmed; the badge is the floor of the flat yards from the ball to the cup). They live in the same plane as the minimap, so map-scale calibration (yards ÷ map pixels) is exact on every hole; elevation only shifts actual carry.
- Each green's height field NEVER changes (player-confirmed) — every heightmap capture of a hole describes the same terrain, so frames fuse across sessions/zooms (kills noise, fills legend-occluded cells).
- Wind indicators (player-confirmed): the minimap is always true-north-up, so its wind arrow is NORTH-relative; the top-right circle arrow is relative to the PLAYER'S AIM — i.e. it directly reads shot-relative wind direction (arrow up = tailwind).

## Truth source (the user's rule, 2026-10-15; NSS since 2026-10-24)
When in doubt, the data decoded from NSS's own game files is the truth: its
collision meshes, course-model bones (cups, tees, trees), placement scenes,
parameter files, code and the values read from its RAM in Eden, each decode
checked by an independent verifier (the dump's `verify/`; a verifier's
correction overrides the decode). Derived and calculated data (scale, green
box/outline, slopes, anything built from captures) is adjusted to match it,
never the other way round. WSR's data (the KCL meshes, PMP placements, main.dol
decodes) was the truth from 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-24 and is now history and
the Dolphin harness's; it is not a fallback (a generator refuses a missing NSS
input rather than fall back to WSR's). Captures confirm and credit (which cup a
round played, the shot rows), never found data of their own: there are no
Switch-only pins.

## Course geometry (NSS's collision — see tools/MESHES.md, do not re-derive)
- NSS's world per hole: metres, y up, −z toward the green (minimap up), +x
  right, the same handedness as the minimap. It is WSR's mesh frame: mesh =
  10·nss + O with a per-hole translation O (`originMesh`, `originMeshY`), no
  rotation (within 0.016°), 9.9979–10.0002 units/m; NSS y 0 = the WSR start's
  height = the terrain's datum. Into the map frame through
  tools/nss-registration.json (the fit of NSS's tree bones on WSR's PMP trees,
  0.008–0.016 px residual); the 250×304 frame is NSS's own minimap capture
  area (GolfMiniMapCaptureParam's AreaCenter lands at 125.4–125.9, 152.7–153.3
  px), and the fit's yd/px equals the scale within 0.0002.
- The collision is Havok 2021.1 `hknpMeshShape` in "Phive" packs, a surface
  code per triangle: 5 GolfFairway, 6 GolfRough, 7 GolfBunker, 8
  GolfWaterhazard, 10 GolfOB, 18 GolfGreen, 24 GolfGreenEdge (fringe), 25
  GolfLeaf, 30 GolfCupside, 31 GolfCup, 32 GolfRock, 34 GolfGlass, 36

  GolfBarrier (35 tee, 38 flag, 42 pin unused in any field mesh). 18.7k–52.6k
  triangles a hole. Heights agree with WSR's KCL to a median ±1.4 mm on 20
  holes (H17 +10.6 mm); the class differences are real course edits (NSS sand
  OB or rock where WSR had water or sea; H19's beach water; walls as rock on 18
  holes).
- Collision outside the field mesh (exported by the dump beside it): the
  TreeLeafNN canopies (GolfLeaf; H8's as 2 cones + 23 spheres; H21's placed only

## Data-entry protocol (why the Log tab looks like it does)
Ended = distance-to-pin(before) − (after); valid at any power if straight, but
ONLY when the aim line points at the pin (angular error θ inflates ended by
~(1−cosθ): negligible under ~2°, ruinous on dogleg default lines). Standard
protocol: Shot Assist ON + the game's suggested line when it points at the
flag (typical approaches); on doglegs either tick the aim onto the flag (a
tick or two off is fine) or skip hand-entering ended — captures stay usable
once map-position measurement lands. Exact power anchors without
gauge-reading: full 4.0 (tee/fairway), maxed rough swing = exact 3.0, maxed
sand swings = 3.0 wedge / 2.0 irons / 1.0 driver-spoon; with Shot Assist all
max swings are trivially exact. Restart-determinism enables controlled
sweeps: restart the track → same wind → test club after club from identical
positions. Sideways miss optional; blank = skipped.
Caution for partial-power rows and logged putts (swing-power.md): NSS's
backswing assist launches a swing at its white line (the backswing's top, which
the gauge marks) when the downswing comes within 0.05 of it, and part way
toward it out to 0.5; the putter assist pulls a putt toward the power its
ghost-ball search found along the aim (a holing one when the line holes), so a
flat putt logged for greenSpeed is cleanest where no assist applies: from the
fringe, beyond 25 m of the cup (30 with Shot Assist), or aimed more than 75°
off the cup. Which power the post-stroke gauge shows against the launch is not
decoded. WSR's hidden pin-power assist on partial swings
(wsr-swing-redzone-spin.md §3.5) is not in NSS's decoded chain.
Import/Export in Log tab takes JSON rows: {club,power,lie,windSpeed,windDeg,ended,side?,hole?,stroke?,landed?,pos?,bearing?,elevFactor?,flatEnded?} (landed: fairway / rough / green / fringe / bunker, where the ball finished; the form's "Finished on"; the last four as derived.json's `shots` carry them, kept when present), or, once putts are logged, {shots, putts, greenSpeed} with putt rows {bars, rolled, flat?, hole?} (greenSpeed is refitted from the rows).

## Roadmap (agreed phases — user wants everything derived silently from uploads)
**Phase 1 — cataloging sweep (done, superseded by the WSR data on 2026-10-16/17 and by NSS's on 2026-10-24/25):** the capture pipeline still does what it was built for — glyph templates from real frames → frame classifier (address / popup / tee-map / green-plain / green-heightmap, hole ID from the "Hole N Par N" banner) → median-stack tee frames → the capture stacks under captures/derived/maps (the comparison art, and the input of render-nss-maps' backdrop) — but the geometry, cups, tee, platform, trees and maps come from NSS's files. The captures confirm them (local badges fit floor(GlfTeeOBJ → cup); the online rounds' badges fit their tier's spots; the green outlines and the 2 m plaid) and supply what the game files cannot: which cup each round played (`seen`) and the shot logs (Phase 2).

## Conventions
- All positions fractional (0–1) relative to the hole map image.
- Never bump storage keys without migration; flags used so far: parFixV3, mapV4, treesV9, mapV5 (map swap + coordinate transform), derivedV1 (derived.json applied wholesale once), clubsWsrV1 (2026-10-21: the seed clubs became WSR's; once, a stored club with no logged shots took them, and a calibrated club's h or c still equal to the guide's seed took WSR's; GUIDE_SEEDS went with clubsNssV1), clubsNssV1 (2026-10-25: the seed clubs became NSS's, and h and c residuals over NSS's flown wind, so a stored club's a, h and c, WSR-era multipliers on the whole wind, would count the wind twice; once, every stored club takes DEFAULT_CLUBS' numbers, its name and maxPower kept, and is refit from its rows as every load does). The auto tree markers are stored without `col` (persist's `storedHolesMeta`; the merge restores it), so that needed no flag. Shot rows: `pos`, `bearing`, `elevFactor`, `flatEnded` are additive; a pipeline row (its id in derived.json `shots`) mirrors derived.json on every load and one listed in `excludedShots` is deleted, so neither needed a flag; old rows' irons (`3i` … `9i`) map to the club ids through CLUB_MIGRATE. Geometry from the game's collision uses a per-hole marker instead of a flag (`green.src` = the hole's `rev`, "nss-…", or a WSR-era "wsr-…" that the NSS geometry re-applies over once; see derivedMerge.js); pipeline pins (`d<hole>-c<k>`; a WSR-era `d<hole>-s*` / `d<hole>-x*` curPin re-keys to its nearest cup on load) and tree markers (`auto: true`, NSS's trees since 2026-10-25, the same `t<hole>-<k>` id form, renumbered) mirror derived.json on every load, so neither needed a flag. Per-hole `tee` and `platform` follow derived.json whole and are never read back. Additive optional keys (e.g. `captures`, the per-hole screenshot catalog; `green.gridEdited`, `green.slopeMaxPct`; the root's `putts` and `greenSpeed`) need no flag. `mapScale` (the solver map's magnification: 2 = fit, 3 / 4 = the map 1.5× / 2× the panel from the 3x / 4x renders) is such a key at the root of the saved state: an install without it reads 2 (`mapScaleOf`), and `persist` writes it with the other root keys. It first chose only the image's resolution at the fitted size; the key and its values did not change when it became the magnification, so no flag. `online` (the root key `{ on, tier, countHigh, countLow, place, hand }`, the Online card's input; src/platform.js `onlineOf` sanitises it, default off / high / 4 + 4 / place 1 / right) is additive too: no flag.
- Generated files are deterministic (fixed decimals and key order, no timestamps, Math.sqrt rather than Math.hypot where the bits must match across engines): two runs give the same bytes, and the tests check it.
- Validate App.jsx with @babel/parser (jsx plugin) before shipping any edit.
- NEVER add a Netlify Form (`data-netlify`) to `index.html`. Netlify registers
  forms by parsing deployed HTML and bills **per submission** — the original
  one-submission-per-photo upload path cost $19 in the Jul–Aug 2026 cycle.
  Capture uploads go through `netlify/edge-functions/upload.js`, which answers
  every POST itself and never calls `context.next()` on one (falling through is
  what would reach Forms). Edge invocations + Blobs are effectively free, so
  upload batch size has no billing consequence.
  Its `config.method` MUST stay `"POST"`: without it the function is invoked
  on every page load, so an upload-path crash serves Netlify's "edge function
  has crashed" page to anyone browsing the site (this happened 2026-08-29).
